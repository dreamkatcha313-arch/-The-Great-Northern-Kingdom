# World, Geography and Identity

## Established
- **Game title:** Axiom Academia.
- **World:** Elyndor.
- Twelve playable class homelands are planned. **Barbarian** and its proposed homeland have been cut; redistribute strength/endurance to Warrior, survival to Ranger, animal/ecological knowledge to Druid, harsh-climate survival to Mage/Northern, and applicable martial conditioning to Samurai.
- Only the Northern Kingdom has a Norse cultural theme. Other regions have distinct aesthetics.
- Yggdrasil lies in/near the Northern Kingdom; Valhalla is near the north. The Central Dojo lies outside the Eastern Kingdom.
- Centerpoint is a central science and trade hub.

## World landmarks and navigation
- **Valhalla:** Planned battle-royale / gladiatorial competition near the north, established in the postwar peace framework.
- **Central Dojo:** PvP site near the Eastern Kingdom.
- **Alliance Realm:** Proposed solo/co-op adventure destination.
- The homepage uses a cinematic map-inspired background, not a surveyed or final navigable world map.
- The raven is a journey vessel/reader in the broader Raven concepts; the in-game selected character's companion depends on class.

## Open geographic work
Coastlines, borders, routes, biomes, distances, and the detailed relative placement of cities and landmarks remain open. Avoid treating illustration labels as definitive cartography.
