# History and Political Conflict

## The Wars of Knowledge — established framework
- Wars were fought over control, access and uses of knowledge.
- **The original cause is deliberately unresolved.** Do not invent one.
- The **Regional Union** included the Western and Umbral kingdoms; other members remain open.
- Valtherus was **not** a Regional Union member.
- After the wars, the **First Academic Accord** established common **Axioms** currency to encourage scholarly exchange and peace. **Valhalla** emerged as a regulated alternative to armed conflict.

## The Night of Falling Embers — Eastern Kingdom
**Working event name and chronology; details may evolve.**
- During the ongoing wars, Western Kingdom operatives launched a devastating surprise incendiary attack on the Eastern capital under Regional Union pressure.
- Civilians were trapped in burning homes, with extensive deaths and destruction.
- Fire sciences, fire suppression and reconstruction become important Eastern studies.
- Advanced **Napalm Magic** is fictional fire magic; historical incendiary technologies belong in separate, accurately attributed educational resources.
- A previously proposed 'Year 417 of the Sealed Age' is **not approved canon**.

## Valtherus wartime atrocities
- An authoritarian Scholar regime used Umbral-derived mental imprisonment, forced labor and extermination facilities including gas chambers.
- The fictional world uses its own event names, institutions and peoples. Do not insert real-world group labels as fictional ethnic identities or transplant real-world events verbatim.
- Separate educational resources can accurately identify and examine real historical atrocities with appropriate sources.
- Treat these acts gravely, never as achievements or player rewards.

## The Severance — established Druid/Ranger history
- The **Verdant Expanse** attempted neutrality during the Wars of Knowledge. Its culture emphasized maintaining forests and developing healing magic.
- An **unknown perpetrator** imposed early mental confinement / psychological magic on the Druids.
- The attack harmed community functioning and disrupted relationships, learning and labor.
- Ranger courts, which had supplied the Druids' judicial services, **denied that such psychological warfare was even possible** against them. The Ranger institutional explanation was **pure incompetence**.
- Judges declared the Druids unfit to care for their **young**, then ordered sweeping removals. This event is called **The Severance**.
- Removal of the young disrupted both **education** (apprenticeships, cultural transmission, skills) and the **working class** (future workers, present workers diverted to appeals and reunification).
- Ranger institutions also struggled: insufficient caregivers, minimal educational provision relative to Ranger young, and failure to cultivate Druid-specific talents.
- Trade with the Druids ended, deepening economic collapse.
- Druid representatives spend much of their time arguing that psychological warfare, judicial intervention and financial warfare may be connected. Their efforts to explain these links are central to their modern politics.
- Across Elyndor, observers speculate about an **undeclared cold war** against the Druids. Motives remain unknown. Other nations committed much greater wartime atrocities yet escaped comparable intervention.
- Druids are recovering and founding specialized schools of parental disciplines, caretaking and health. Valtherus now leads formal medical knowledge, while Druids retain ecological and healing-magic specialties.
- The Druids are creating **Verdants** to rebuild economic autonomy.

### Preserve ambiguity
**Established:** the magical attack occurred; Ranger authorities denied its possibility; the young were removed; society and trade collapsed.  
**Unresolved:** who cast the magic, why, whether financial warfare was coordinated, and who benefited.  
The rumor of a cold war is not proof of a specific conspiracy. Players may investigate conflicting records and accounts.

## Mental confinement and institutional accountability
- Umbral's Houses of Silence exist within minds; Shadow Asylum was a physical research facility.
- Coercive Concentrated Awareness experiments at Shadow Asylum were presented as healing.
- Political conflicts include whether mental imprisonment should be used, hearings conducted without a prisoner's awareness, malnutrition during confinement, lack of aftercare, and the burden placed on survivors to repair harm.
- **Silence Spell:** fictional effect that may follow a psychosis-inducing mental spell, temporarily disrupting communication/verbal magic.
