# Gameplay, Classes, Mana and Skills

## Core player model
- Axiom Academia is envisioned as a cross-platform educational fantasy MMORPG, **not yet implemented as an MMO**.
- Multiple character slots; class switching is planned.
- Account-wide educational knowledge and character-specific class mastery are design goals.
- Players should eventually combine abilities from **at least three class codices**.
- Educational lessons remain available independently of combat progression; assessment can gate use/equipping of some items and abilities.

## Codices and companions
Class-specific codices track study and abilities. Spirit companions are region/class specific (see [REGIONS.md](REGIONS.md)). The homepage currently previews **Jade**, a Mage, with **Muninn**; this is not an authenticated identity.

## Concentrated Awareness — working skill model
A proposed skill progression:
1. **Attention:** sustain focus on selected stimuli or tasks.
2. **Recognition:** notice patterns and internal responses.
3. **Regulation:** manage attention and response to distraction.
4. **Projection:** translate concentrated awareness into fictional magic.
5. **Manifestation:** advanced simulated mental constructs.

Applications vary by class: Northern Mage introspection and illusion; Umbral mental projection and coercion; Druid regulation, recovery and healing magic. This is **gameplay design**, not a validated medical protocol.

## Mana theory — open research
- Mana is expected to have **multiple subtypes**, not a single bioelectric explanation.
- Bioelectricity/galvanism is one real-world inspiration for an Umbral branch, not evidence of reanimation or a complete mana theory.
- Define mana types, resource costs, regeneration, interaction rules and balance in future specifications.

## Conflict and combat
- **Valhalla:** planned battle-royale/gladiatorial competition.
- **Central Dojo:** planned PvP training and duels.
- **Alliance Realm:** proposed solo/cooperative encounters.
- Eastern fire systems may affect vegetation and buildings in unprotected zones. Protected settlements need peacekeeper response, evacuation and arson consequences.
- Advanced fictional fire spells and historically documented weapons should be distinguished in educational content.
- Character ability sets, combat engines, server validation and matchmaking are **not implemented**.

## Learning design
- Build short lessons, longer references, assessments, class-specific guides and practice sequences.
- Mark established evidence versus emerging or speculative claims in relevant health content.
- Maintain historical accuracy in learning material, even when in-game nations/events use original fictional identities.
