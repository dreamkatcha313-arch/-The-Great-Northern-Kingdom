# Economy, Stores and Progression

## Established or selected
- **Axioms:** shared world currency established after the First Academic Accord.
- **Verdants:** selected name for the Druid homeland's new recovery currency.
- **The Exchange of Elyndor:** central marketplace.
- **The Shadow Exchange:** Western Kingdom's regional store.
- Regionally themed currencies and shops are planned for each homeland.

## Working proposals
- **Aether Shards:** potential premium currency; not finalized.
- Players may acquire equipment, outfits, cosmetic schemes, educational materials and class-relevant gear.
- Knowledge assessments can govern equipping/using certain items; purchased access should not imply demonstrated mastery.
- Educational material and account knowledge are envisioned to persist across devices, requiring secure server-side systems.

## Druid reconstruction economy
The Verdant Expanse seeks to issue Verdants for local trade, education, forest stewardship and rebuilding after the Severance. Adoption, convertibility and external recognition are unresolved. The world-wide Axiom economy remains in place.

## Not implemented
No secure payment processing, live store inventory, real account ledger, cross-device ownership, exchange-rate engine, authenticated characters or server-side progress. Homepage store/exchange controls are informational prototypes.
