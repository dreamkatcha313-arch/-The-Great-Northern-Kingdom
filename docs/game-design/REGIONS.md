# Class Homelands and Educational Curricula

## Mage — Northern Kingdom (approved regional concept)
- Norse-inspired halls, Yggdrasil, psychology, diagnoses, mentalism, perception, phobias, illusion and mental projection.
- **Muninn:** external raven spirit companion. **Huginn:** internal spirit raven linked to the Book of Shadows and spring studies.
- Psychological theories and IFS-related explorations may connect to the separate Winter Raven educational project.
- Northern dark history includes unethical, unwitting-subject psychological research; the name **Veiled Mind Initiative** is working, not canon.

## Samurai — Eastern Kingdom (approved regional concept)
- Wolf spirit; **Fivefold Codex** and **Fivefold Disciplines**.
- Earth: foundation; Water: adaptability; Fire: engagement; Wind: comparative traditions; Void: mastery.
- Studies include historical Niten Ichi-ryū, Miyamoto Musashi's *Book of Five Rings*, Bushidō, strategy, movement and physical training.
- **Ember Academy / School of Fire Sciences**: combustion, fire safety, suppression, rescue, and fictional fire magic.
- Eastern Kingdom was attacked by Western forces during the ongoing Wars of Knowledge.

## Ninja — Western Kingdom
- **Shade**, dark smoke companion; **Codex of Veils**; **Shadow Exchange** regional store.
- Historical shinobi, stealth, intelligence, observation, cryptography, strategy, movement, blackpowder/firearms history and fictional illusions.
- Western Kingdom was drawn into the Regional Union and pressured to develop advanced fire magic and launch an incendiary surprise attack on the east. Its factions disagree about the legacy.

## Necromancer — Umbral Kingdom
- Shadow spirit companion; anatomy, death, consciousness, id/ego, mentalist studies, galvanism and bioelectric phenomena.
- **Hollow Reanimation:** animated remains without original consciousness (fictional).
- **Instinctive Reanimation:** proposed recovery of an 'Id Echo' (fictional).
- Possession and master healing/revival are advanced fictional mechanics.
- **Inner Dominion:** Leaders, Soldiers and Exiles as mental entities in magical mental simulations.
- **Houses of Silence:** mental facilities *inside minds*, not physical buildings.
- **Shadow Asylum:** physical abandoned research institution. Researchers tried coercive Concentrated Awareness as purported healing, producing harms including psychotic states and silence.

## Scholar — Valtherus
- Moth companion. Formal medical knowledge, anatomy, physiology, clinical research, archives, philosophy, history, logic, ethics, mathematics, critical thinking and academic authority.
- Not a member of the Regional Union.
- Its authoritarian wartime regime used mental imprisonment techniques acquired from Umbral, forced labor and extermination facilities; detailed in [HISTORY.md](HISTORY.md).
- Its position as Elyndor's formal medical authority can conflict with Druid traditions.

## Druid — Verdant Expanse
- Elvish aesthetic; fox companion; forest stewardship, ecology, botanical traditions, environmental restoration and healing magic.
- Before the Severance, the civilization maintained forests and healing knowledge and attempted neutrality.
- After the Severance, rebuilding includes specialized education in parental disciplines, caregiving, health and cultural continuity.
- Valtherus, **not** the Druids, now dominates formal medical scholarship; Druid specialties remain distinct.
- **Verdants** is the selected emerging currency. See [HISTORY.md](HISTORY.md) and [ECONOMY.md](ECONOMY.md).

## Ranger — Warden's Reach (working name)
- Stag companion; law, investigation, justice, legal institutions, wilderness survival and stewardship.
- Ranger courts administered Druid judicial services, rejected the possibility of the magical psychological attack and attributed Druid difficulties to incompetence. They ordered mass removal of the Druid **young**.
- Ranger institutions were not prepared to nurture the young; educational inequality and cultural mismatch followed.
- Region's legal institutions and accountability conflicts remain major narrative themes.

## Warrior — Ironhold (working name)
- Bear companion; strength, endurance, conditioning, martial defense and tactics.
- Absorbs Barbarian physical-training material.

## Cleric — Southern Kingdom
- Living-light spirit companion; theology, scripture, ethics, healing and religious studies, with the Bible as an important real-world educational reference.
- Keep other regions culturally distinct; do not make the entire world overtly religious.

## Alchemist — Centerpoint
- Salamander companion; chemistry, physics, atomic structure, experimentation, scientific knowledge, trade and future portal/teleportation studies.
- Centerpoint is a scientific hub; scripture is notably absent from its academic program, a point for NPC discussion.

## Bard — Resonant Isles (working name)
- Songbird companion; music, language, literature and creative expression.

## Artificer — Forgeward Dominion (working name)
- Clockwork owl companion; engineering, mechanics, invention and robotics.

## Curriculum rules
Lessons should distinguish verifiable real-world information from fictional magic. Practical historical forms should be sourced and presented with suitable training context. Individual regional curricula are still outlines, not finished courses.
